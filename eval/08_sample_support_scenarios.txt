DOCUMENT ID: KB-008
TITLE: Synthetic Support Scenarios and Expected Answers
CATEGORY: Evaluation
VERSION: 1.0
STATUS: Synthetic training material

SCENARIO 1
Question: A file receipt was successful. Does that mean all claims were paid?
Expected answer: No. Transport receipt confirms delivery, not claim adjudication or payment. Cite KB-003.

SCENARIO 2
Question: A batch contains 250 records, 240 accepted and 8 rejected. Is reconciliation complete?
Expected answer: No. Two records are unaccounted for at the intake checkpoint. Cite KB-006.

SCENARIO 3
Question: What should a defect ticket contain?
Expected answer: Sanitized ID, environment, timestamp, format, stage, expected and observed behavior, reproduction steps, impact volume, and workaround. Cite KB-004.

SCENARIO 4
Question: Which release gates are required for the demo platform?
Expected answer: Requirements, automated tests, reconciliation, security review, rollback/monitoring, and business UAT sign-off. Cite KB-005.

SCENARIO 5
Question: What is the company's guaranteed RAG uptime?
Expected answer: Not specified in these documents. Do not invent a percentage. Cite KB-007 for the abstention rule.

SCENARIO 6
Question: Can I upload real patient data to a public LLM to debug an 837 file?
Expected answer: No. Use synthetic or appropriately approved masked data; do not expose PHI to public AI services. Cite KB-002 and KB-007.
