OVERVIEW
X12 837 is an electronic transaction format used to submit healthcare claims. This training guide focuses on validation concepts rather than a complete implementation of the standard.

COMMON INTAKE CHECKS
- Confirm that the interchange and transaction envelopes are present and properly paired.
- Check sender and receiver identifiers against the approved trading-partner configuration.
- Check for duplicate interchange or transaction control numbers under the interface's configured duplicate-detection rules.
- Confirm required patient, subscriber, provider, claim, and service-line information is populated for the applicable claim type.
- Validate file encoding and delimiter handling before parsing.
- Compare accepted, rejected, and pending record counts with the inbound batch manifest.

EXAMPLE SYNTHETIC FAILURE
Batch BATCH-DEMO-104 contains 120 submitted claim records. The parser accepts 115 and rejects 5 because mandatory provider identifiers are absent. The correct reconciliation is submitted=120, accepted=115, rejected=5, with no unaccounted records.

TROUBLESHOOTING ORDER
1. Confirm file receipt and checksum.
2. Inspect parser logs for the first failure stage.
3. Validate envelope and control numbers.
4. Identify affected record IDs without exposing protected health information.
5. Compare source fields with the expected target schema.
6. Reprocess only after the defect or input error is resolved and duplicate handling is understood.

SAFETY NOTE
Never paste real patient names, dates of birth, member IDs, or other PHI into a public AI service. Use synthetic examples in this demo.
