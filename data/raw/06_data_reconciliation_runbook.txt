TITLE: Data Reconciliation Runbook
CATEGORY: Data Quality
VERSION: 1.0
STATUS: Synthetic training material

CORE FORMULA
Submitted records = accepted records + rejected records + unaccounted records, assuming each submitted record is assigned to exactly one category at the intake checkpoint.

EXAMPLE A: PASS
Submitted 250; accepted 242; rejected 8; unaccounted 0. Reconciliation passes.

EXAMPLE B: FAIL
Submitted 250; accepted 240; rejected 8; unaccounted 2. Reconciliation fails. Investigate the 2 unaccounted records before closing the batch.

COMMON CAUSES OF MISMATCH
Duplicate handling, parser failures, dropped records, unexpected filtering, asynchronous processing delays, and mismatched batch boundaries.

INVESTIGATION STEPS
1. Confirm the checkpoint and time window used by each count.
2. Compare batch IDs and record identifiers across stages.
3. Separate in-flight records from missing records.
4. Check duplicate and retry logs.
5. Document the root cause and rerun the reconciliation after correction.

IMPORTANT
A claim pending adjudication can still count as accepted at the intake checkpoint. Do not classify every pending claim as unaccounted.
